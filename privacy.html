Terms & Privacy

This is a pre-launch site. We're not currently selling anything or processing payments — this page mainly covers the email list you can join below, and sets out the basics for when we do launch.

Using this site

This website is provided as-is, to share information about The Full Deck and let you sign up to hear about launch. Content, pricing, and product details are subject to change before launch. Nothing on this site is a contract or an offer of sale.

What we collect

If you submit the email signup form, we collect your email address. We don't take accounts or payment details.

If you say yes to analytics cookies, we also use Google Analytics to measure visits. Google receives your IP address (used to work out roughly where you are, not stored by Analytics), your browser and device type, the pages you view, how you got to the site, and what you tap on, such as which games you open. We use this only to see how the site is used, and we don't use advertising features or build advertising audiences. If you say no, none of this is collected.

Why we collect it

Solely to send you a one-time notification when The Full Deck is ready to order. We won't send you a recurring newsletter or marketing emails beyond that, and we won't sell or share your email with anyone outside the email service we use to send it.

How long we keep it

Until we've sent that launch notification, or until you unsubscribe — whichever happens first.

Your rights

  • You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any point by contacting us (see below).
  • You can ask what data we hold about you at any time.

Cookies

We only set cookies if you choose Accept on the cookie banner. Until then, no analytics cookies are set and nothing is sent to Google.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_BNTY8C42YG in your browser, which last up to two years, to tell visits apart. Google may process this data on servers outside the UK.

You can change your mind at any time: use here or in the footer of any page. Choosing Reject switches analytics off and removes the cookies. We ask again after a year.

Contact

Questions about your data or this page — email us at hello@thefulldeck.com.
